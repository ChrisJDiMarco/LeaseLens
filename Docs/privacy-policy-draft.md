# LeaseLens Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: [set before publication]

LeaseLens helps people review public NYC housing complaint and violation records for an address they choose to search.

## Information Used

When you enter an address, LeaseLens sends that search query directly from your device to NYC Planning GeoSearch and NYC Open Data endpoints to retrieve public records. Version 1.0 does not require an account and does not operate a LeaseLens server that stores your search history.

## Tracking and Advertising

LeaseLens version 1.0 does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, analytics SDKs, or third-party marketing tools.

## Third-Party Public Data Services

Search requests are processed by public NYC data services. Their handling of network requests is governed by their applicable policies and practices. LeaseLens displays links to the public-data sources used in each report.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Because LeaseLens version 1.0 does not create accounts or store search history on a LeaseLens server, there is no account data or stored search history for us to delete. Removing the app removes app-local cached network responses under iOS storage management.

## Important Disclaimer

Public complaint and violation records may be incomplete, unconfirmed, duplicated, or no longer reflect current conditions. LeaseLens is not legal advice and does not guarantee the condition of a building.
